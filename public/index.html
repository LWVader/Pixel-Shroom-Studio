Artwork Use, Authenticity, and Ownership

Clear answers about using, protecting, and verifying your purchase.

Is a separate license included with my purchase?

No. Pixel Shroom Studio does not require a separate usage license. After purchase, the buyer may use the downloaded artwork freely.

How may I use purchased artwork?

Purchased artwork may be used for personal or commercial purposes. Buyers may display, print, publish, modify, resize, reproduce, incorporate, redistribute, or resell the artwork as they see fit.

Does Pixel Shroom Studio claim copyright over the artwork?

Pixel Shroom Studio does not assert copyright restrictions over its purchased AI-generated artwork. The artwork is sold for unrestricted use by the buyer.

Copyright treatment of AI-generated material can vary by country and may depend on the amount of human contribution involved. Pixel Shroom Studio makes no promise that a buyer can obtain exclusive copyright protection for a purchased image.

Does purchasing an artwork give me exclusive rights?

No, unless the listing explicitly identifies the sale as exclusive. Other customers may purchase or use copies of the same artwork.

The cryptographic signature verifies the file’s source and integrity; it does not create exclusive copyright or prevent other copies from existing.

Can I use a purchased NFT however I choose?

Yes. Buyers may use, display, modify, reproduce, publish, redistribute, or resell purchased NFT artwork as they see fit.

Blockchain ownership, artwork usage, and copyright status are separate concepts. Purchasing an NFT does not guarantee that copyright protection is available or that similar images do not exist elsewhere.

What makes my original authentic?

The delivered original includes a Pixel Shroom Studio serial identity, cryptographic signature, SHA-256 fingerprint, and authenticity record. These elements help confirm that the file was issued by Pixel Shroom Studio and has not been changed since it was signed.

What does the signed original provide me?

Your purchase includes the original unwatermarked artwork with its assigned LWV serial number, cryptographic signature, and SHA-256 fingerprint. Together, these records confirm that the file was issued by Pixel Shroom Studio, matches its registered artwork and serial number, and has not been altered since signing.

Your receipt and purchase record show that you obtained the artwork from Pixel Shroom Studio. Keep the original unchanged and use separate copies for resizing, editing, printing, sharing, or AI enhancement.

Can I resize or modify the artwork?

Yes. Buyers are free to resize, edit, enhance, crop, convert, or otherwise modify their purchased artwork.

Any modification creates a derivative file with a different digital fingerprint. The modified version will no longer pass verification as the unchanged signed original.

Can AI reproduce the artwork at a different size?

Yes. AI tools or conventional editing software may create a different-sized version while the signed original is retained separately.

The new version is a derivative and will not carry the original file’s valid fingerprint unless it is signed separately.

Should I preserve the original download?

Yes. Keep the original signed file unchanged and create separate copies for editing, printing, resizing, sharing, or AI enhancement. Preserving the original allows its serial, signature, and cryptographic fingerprint to remain verifiable.

Does the cryptographic signature prevent copying?

No. Cryptographic signing does not prevent copying, screenshots, redistribution, or modification. It provides a way to confirm the source and integrity of the unchanged original.

Will my purchased image contain watermarks?

No. Website previews are protected with full-image watermarks before purchase. The delivered original is provided without the storefront watermark.

Why is the website preview different from the purchased file?

Website previews may be resized, compressed, converted, and watermarked for display and protection. The purchased file is the private, unwatermarked original described in the listing.

How long is the download available?

Download links are temporary and may have a download limit. Buyers should download the file promptly and keep secure backup copies.

What happens if I lose my file?

Contact Pixel Shroom Studio using the email address associated with the order. Purchase verification may be required before replacement access can be provided.

Are digital purchases refundable?

Because downloaded digital files cannot be returned, completed digital purchases are generally final except where required by law or when the delivered file is defective or materially different from its listing.
