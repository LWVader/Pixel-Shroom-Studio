Before you buy

A few clear answers.

What happens after payment is confirmed?

Once the server verifies payment, the concealed card starts spinning on the forest reveal screen. It keeps moving while your assigned image is prepared and loaded, then slows smoothly into the reveal. There is one continuous opening, without a stationary waiting card or a second roll. Reduced-motion settings disable spinning. The animation presents the server’s result; it never chooses the tier.

What does Add to my collection do?

After reveal, select Add to my collection. It saves the edition to your signed-in account and starts downloads of the exact minted image and signed JSON receipt. Your browser may ask you to allow multiple downloads or choose where to save them. Keep both files and a backup. Already-saved editions offer file downloads again without another collection entry.

Can I request any studio artwork on canvas or apparel?

Use the contact form to send the artwork title or LWV serial and the product you want. LWVader will check print suitability, available Gelato product variations and pricing. A request does not guarantee that a product or destination can be supported.
