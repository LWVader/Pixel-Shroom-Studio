Before you buy

A few clear answers.

What arrives after a digital purchase?

A watermark-free original file, delivered privately after verified payment. Check the artwork’s registered format and pixel dimensions before purchasing, then save your master file and a backup.

Can I use the artwork commercially?

Yes. Personal and commercial use, editing, redistribution and resale are included under the studio’s published usage terms. Purchases are nonexclusive unless a listing explicitly states otherwise.

Can I request another design on canvas or apparel?

Yes. Send the artwork title or LWV serial number and the product you have in mind through the contact form. We’ll discuss available options and pricing. Physical purchases take place on eBay.
