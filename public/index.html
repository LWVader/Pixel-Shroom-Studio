FAQ

Good to know.

What can I use the visuals for?

Tell me where the campaign will run. I can compose visuals for social posts, ads, banners, thumbnails, or website hero sections.

Is this a logo or branding package?

The Attention Pack includes five campaign images. Send any existing logo, palette, or brand guidelines as links in your brief.

How do I plan around a launch date?

Put your launch date in the brief. For a time-sensitive campaign, email the studio before ordering to confirm availability.

How do I add a landing page?

Purchase an Attention Pack and submit your brief. The thank-you page offers a separate $199 checkout for a matching one-page landing page. Hosting and domain purchases are not included.

What is included in the Monthly Asset Pack?

Ten new, randomly themed AI-generated social backgrounds for $49 USD per month. Images are independent, not coordinated or customized to your brand. No creative brief is required. Your files are delivered by email; add your own text, logo, or product images. Manage your subscription through Stripe when online checkout is available.

Who makes the images?

I’m Loren, the digital artist behind Pixel Shroom Studio and LWVader. I use AI image tools and my own art direction to develop the concept and prepare the finished files.
