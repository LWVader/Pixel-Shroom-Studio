Before you collect

A few clear answers.

What can I do with my purchased artwork or NFT?

You may use your purchase for personal or commercial projects. You may display, print, publish, modify, resize, reproduce, incorporate, redistribute, or resell the artwork as you see fit. Pixel Shroom Studio does not require a separate usage license after purchase. Keep the signed original unchanged as your verifiable master file, and make separate copies for editing, printing, sharing, or AI enhancement.

Can I use the artwork commercially?

Yes. Purchased artwork and NFTs may be used in commercial projects, advertising, websites, publications, products, presentations, prints, and other work. Pixel Shroom Studio does not charge an additional commercial-use fee.

Can I modify, resize, or enhance the artwork?

Yes. You may crop, resize, recolor, convert, enhance, combine, or otherwise modify your purchase. Any changed version becomes a derivative file with a different digital fingerprint and will not verify as the unchanged signed original.
