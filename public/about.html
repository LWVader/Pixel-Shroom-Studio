Artist & creator

Meet LWVADER.

Hi, I’m LWVader, the creator behind Pixel Shroom Studio. I combine imagination, AI tools and digital artistry to create distinctive designs and artwork, from fantasy worlds and unforgettable characters to canvas prints and apparel.

Why I Created Pixel Shroom Studio

Art With Identity. Creativity With Proof.

As an artist and creator, I see thousands of AI-generated images flooding the internet every day. But here’s a question worth asking: When you purchase digital artwork, how do you know where it actually came from?

Is it an original creation? Or is it a copy of a copy, slightly altered, repackaged and sold again with no clear connection to the person who first imagined it?

That’s exactly why I created Pixel Shroom Studio and the LWVADER artwork identification system.

I believe digital artwork deserves more than just a beautiful image. It deserves an identity, a history and a traceable connection to its creator.

Each registered artwork has a studio identity. For new numbered collections, I upload six finished tier images and prepare individually identified copies. Each released edition has its own number, file fingerprint and signed receipt, connecting the exact delivered file to its studio record.

Whether you’re purchasing artwork for advertising, canvas prints, apparel, digital displays or creative projects, I want you to have confidence in what you’re buying.

Not just another anonymous AI-generated image, but an individually identified creation with documented provenance.

My goal isn’t simply to generate and sell artwork. It’s to bring accountability, transparency and a sense of authenticity to a digital art world where images can be copied, modified and redistributed in seconds.

AI may be the tool I use, but the imagination, creative direction and vision behind each piece are my own.

Because art should have more than a price tag. It should have an identity, a creator and a story you can trace.

— LWVADER

What the proof establishes.

Edition verification checks an unchanged delivered file against its signed receipt and the studio’s trusted public key. Earlier direct-file originals use their LWV serial and original verification record. Neither method establishes exclusive ownership, copyright ownership or the absence of similar artwork. Existing personal and commercial permissions remain in place.

Keep the issued file and its receipt unchanged, and edit separate copies. A physical print or modified image cannot match the exact bytes of the issued digital file.
