What the percentages mean.

Base odds: Standard 60%, Silver 20%, Gold 10%, Emerald 6%, Sapphire 3%, Prismatic 1%. Standard, Silver and Gold share the same thick canvas. Emerald or above has a 10% combined base chance of framing, and Prismatic has a 1% base chance of the 24 × 24 size. Remaining tier caps can change effective odds.

Secure server-side randomness assigns the tier after verified payment. The animation presents the result. No outcome is generated on-chain, and secure randomness alone is not a publicly verifiable fairness proof.

Listings have authorized supply and tier caps. Exhausted tiers downgrade under the listing's rules, so effective odds change. A Forge output consumes authorized next-tier stock too. Check the artwork's current disclosures rather than assuming a rare result remains available.
