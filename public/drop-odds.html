How the odds work.

The planned base rates are Standard 60%, Silver 20%, Gold 10%, Emerald 6%, Sapphire 3% and Prismatic 1%. Silver shares Standard’s slim canvas; Gold and above change the physical format. No outcomes are generated on-chain. A cryptographic server roll is not, by itself, a publicly verifiable fairness proof.

Current edition listings disclose their own rules and remaining caps. Effective odds can change as caps fill. The proposed guarantees below need reserved inventory and server enforcement before launch; a paid promise cannot be silently downgraded.
