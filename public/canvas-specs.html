A canvas roll already includes its print.

The planned Canvas Mystery Roll costs $69 + $13.49 prepaid shipping, before applicable tax. It includes one digital image and one canvas claim. Hold the print until redemption; do not pay this later-print table again. A Prismatic canvas-roll result does not grant an extra second free canvas.

Print-ready is more than a DPI tag.

A 16-inch square needs 4800 × 4800 pixels for 300 PPI; a 16 × 20 portrait needs 4800 × 6000 before additional bleed and wrap. The exact Gelato template controls final production dimensions. Digital file specifications are shown on each listing; not every file is 4K PNG.

Protected previews are not print masters. The owner’s preflight checks file fit and resolution, with visual review for safe areas, edges and color. Delivery estimates come from the actual order, not a blanket 3–5-day promise.
