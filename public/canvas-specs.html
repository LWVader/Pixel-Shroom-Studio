Canvas already included?

The Canvas + Digital Roll costs $69 + $13.49 prepaid shipping before applicable tax. It includes one digital variant and one canvas claim. Redeem it once from My Vault with no second product or shipping charge. Hold the claim until you are ready; it cannot be combined in the Forge or transferred.

See the composition before printing.

Emerald and Sapphire use a dedicated 16 × 24 portrait composition; the other tiers use square canvases. Review the saved print composition and choose an available frame before redeeming or ordering. Products are fulfilled in the US through their exact Gelato product mappings.

At 300 PPI, a 16 × 16 print area requires 4800 × 4800 pixels, 16 × 24 requires 4800 × 7200 pixels, and 24 × 24 requires 7200 × 7200 before any product-specific margins. The editor checks the actual cropped source detail.

Production & delivery.

Confirm the delivery address and product in My Vault. Gelato receives the dedicated private print source only through the consented quote/order workflow. Track production and any available shipment link in My Vault → Orders. Estimates depend on the actual product and destination.
