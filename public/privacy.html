Privacy

Privacy notice

Pixel Shroom Studio respects the privacy of people who visit this website and contact the studio.

Information you provide

When you send a message through the contact form, the studio receives the name, email address, subject, and message you choose to provide. This information is used to respond to your request.

Purchases

Stripe handles payment-card details. Studio accounts, orders, edition allocations, signed receipts, collection visibility and marketplace transfers are recorded to provide purchases and check access. Physical-order shipping details are shared with Gelato when you request a quote and place an order. Gelato receives a temporary private link to a dedicated print-ready file for fulfillment. Physical orders do not send your digital-edition files automatically. Stripe receives the delivery details used for physical checkout and tax calculation.

Edition verification

The edition verifier hashes the selected image locally in your browser and checks its signed receipt against the studio’s public signing key. The selected image and receipt are not uploaded for this verification. Your browser retrieves the trusted public key from the studio service.

Cookies and technical information

This website may use essential technical storage needed to operate the site and its contact form. See the cookie notice for more information.

Contact

For a privacy question, email lwvader@pixelshroomstudio.com.

Optional email updates

If you request studio news or NFT release notifications, we store your name, email address, chosen updates and consent request so the studio can manage the emails you asked for. Signup requests are handled personally by the studio. You can unsubscribe by replying to an update or contacting lwvader@pixelshroomstudio.com.
