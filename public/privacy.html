Campaign privacy

Checkout and briefs

Stripe collects your payment details. Pixel Shroom Studio receives the email you use at checkout and a Stripe reference for your order. Your campaign brief asks for your business name, industry, preferences, and launch details.

How we use your information

We use these details to confirm your order, make your campaign visuals, and contact you about your delivery. Briefs and email delivery status are stored in Cloudflare D1. We send the finished ZIP to the email address you used at checkout.
