Artwork and purchases

Review each listing’s price, format, image dimensions, supply, tier odds and caps before paying. Numbered editions use verified Stripe payment followed by server-side allocation and reveal. Numbered editions and marketplace transfers

A primary numbered-edition purchase assigns an available tier under its listing’s published rules; no particular rare tier is guaranteed. A replay or delivery retry does not create another roll. You receive the assigned tier copy and signed receipt, not the private master or exclusive rights to the design. Marketplace purchases transfer an existing edition’s recorded holder after verified payment, without rerolling. Previously downloaded copies cannot be recalled. Applicable seller onboarding, listed fees and payout requirements are disclosed in the marketplace. These internal ledger editions are not blockchain tokens, and no resale value is promised.

Forge & Print editions

For enabled new offers, a Digital Roll costs $8 before applicable tax. Canvas + Digital costs $69 plus $13.49 prepaid US shipping before applicable tax and includes one canvas claim. Each artwork discloses the available offer and listing-specific rules before payment.

Forge consumes the confirmed eligible digital-only edition records in exchange for the stated next-tier copy, subject to authorized output inventory. Consumed editions cannot be downloaded, printed or resold through the studio again. Previously downloaded files and published permissions cannot be revoked. Editions attached to prepaid canvas claims or print orders are excluded from this release's Forge. Digital-only upgrades do not include a free print.

Included claims are redeemable once for the associated artwork and tier in the US, with no second product or shipping charge. They cannot be transferred or combined. Bundles and promotional free prints are not enabled in this release. Existing purchases keep their stated price and delivery promises.
