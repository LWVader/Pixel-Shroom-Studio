What transfers?

The existing numbered edition changes registered holder after payment verification. Its image and studio-signed receipt stay the same. The former holder loses access to new downloads through this site; already downloaded copies cannot be erased. Existing personal and commercial permissions are unchanged.

Seller listings show their price. The platform takes 10% of the sale price and pays Stripe processing fees from its share; seller payouts follow Stripe availability and account requirements. Resales have no promised future value.
