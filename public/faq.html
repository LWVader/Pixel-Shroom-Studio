Buyer information

Your edition. Your questions answered.

From choosing artwork to revealing, saving, verifying and trading a numbered edition.

What is a numbered digital edition?

A numbered edition is one privately delivered copy of a studio-uploaded tier image, with its own edition number, prepared file fingerprint and studio-signed receipt. It is recorded in the studio’s internal collection ledger. It does not include the private master asset or a blockchain token. “Minting” here means issuing that numbered copy in the studio ledger, not creating a blockchain asset.

Which tiers are available?

New six-tier collections use Standard, Silver, Gold, Emerald, Sapphire and Prismatic. LWVader uploads a finished image for each tier before the collection can open. The purchased file uses the assigned tier image; the reveal does not generate an overlay or alter the artwork’s visible pixels. Check the listing for its available tiers, image sizes, supply and caps. Older collections may use an earlier tier structure.

Can I choose my tier before paying?

A primary edition purchase assigns a tier on the server after verified payment. You choose the artwork, not the tier. Review the listing’s published initial odds and tier caps before checkout. A secondary marketplace purchase is different: you buy the specific tier and edition already shown in that listing.

How do odds, supply and tier caps work?

New six-tier collections start with Standard 60%, Silver 20%, Gold 10%, Emerald 6%, Sapphire 3% and Prismatic 1%, unless the listing shows another supported rule set. The server uses secure randomness and atomically reserves one prepared copy. Each collection has a maximum supply and caps on its tiers. If a rolled tier is exhausted, allocation moves down to an available lower tier under the listing’s rules, so effective probabilities change as caps fill. The listing’s current disclosures apply; a rare tier is never guaranteed.

What happens after payment is confirmed?

Once the server verifies payment, the concealed card starts spinning on the forest reveal screen. It keeps moving while your assigned image is prepared and loaded, then slows smoothly into the reveal. There is one continuous opening, without a stationary waiting card or a second roll. Reduced-motion settings disable spinning. The animation presents the server’s result; it never chooses the tier.

What files and dimensions will I receive?

You receive a numbered copy of the assigned finished tier image in its listed PNG, JPEG or WebP format, plus a signed JSON receipt. Check the tier image dimensions before paying; they can differ from the protected master preview. Copy-identification metadata gives prepared copies distinct file bytes and SHA-256 fingerprints while preserving the uploaded image’s visible pixels. The delivery keeps any lettering or design elements included in that finished artwork; the storefront’s protective preview watermark is not added to the purchase.

What does Add to my collection do?

After reveal, select Add to my collection. It saves the edition to your signed-in account and starts downloads of the exact minted image and signed JSON receipt. Your browser may ask you to allow multiple downloads or choose where to save them. Keep both files and a backup. Already-saved editions offer file downloads again without another collection entry.

What if preparation, a download or my connection fails?

Your paid allocation keeps the same edition number, tier and prepared copy on retries; it is not rolled again. Keep your confirmation link. Sign in to My collection and use the purchase waiting to add to reopen the reveal when available. If collection saving succeeded but a download failed, retry downloading the same edition’s files. Contact the studio with your order details if processing needs attention.

Do I need an account or a crypto wallet?

Sign in or create a studio account before purchasing a numbered edition. The account stores your collection and lets the site confirm the current holder for downloads and marketplace actions. You do not need a crypto wallet or blockchain transaction for these editions.

How do I verify my numbered edition?

Open Verify an edition and select the unchanged downloaded image and its signed JSON receipt. Your browser calculates the file’s SHA-256 fingerprint and checks the receipt with the studio’s trusted public signing key. The image is not uploaded for this check. A match confirms the file and studio-signed receipt; it does not prove current ownership, exclusivity or resale value.

Do copies of the same tier have different hashes?

For new prepared-copy collections, each authorized copy has its own identity metadata and file fingerprint before checkout opens. A paid allocation releases that reserved copy and its signed receipt. Copies can look identical while their file bytes and hashes differ. A hash is calculated from file bytes; it cannot be assigned a different value without a byte change. Earlier delivered files keep their original verification method.

What if a listing sells a direct digital file instead?

A direct-file listing uses its existing checkout and private download process. It does not include a random tier reveal or a numbered-edition receipt unless its listing explicitly says so. For an earlier studio-issued original, use the original-file verification page with its LWV serial. Follow the format, delivery limits and permissions shown on that listing.

Can I use, modify, redistribute or resell the artwork?

Personal and commercial use, editing, printing, reproduction, redistribution and resale remain permitted under the published Usage Rights. Keep the issued file and receipt unchanged and edit separate copies, because changed bytes produce a different fingerprint. Redistributing a file does not transfer its recorded holder in the studio’s collection ledger.

Does a limited edition give me exclusive rights?

No, unless a listing explicitly states otherwise. Edition supply limits the studio-issued numbered copies for that collection; it does not make the underlying artwork exclusive or prevent copying. A serial, hash or receipt verifies the issued file’s identity and integrity, not exclusive copyright or ownership of every copy.

How does the secondary marketplace work?

When marketplace selling is available, an eligible current holder can complete Stripe seller onboarding and list a ready edition from My collection. Buyers purchase that existing tier and edition; there is no new rarity roll. After verified payment, the studio ledger transfers the recorded holder and site download access. The image and signed receipt stay the same, and previously downloaded files cannot be recalled. The current platform fee is 10%; Stripe processing fees are paid from the platform’s share. Seller payouts follow Stripe requirements. No sale or future resale value is guaranteed.

Can I buy canvas prints, apparel or gifts?

Yes. Canvas and apparel pages show studio-mapped products fulfilled by Gelato. Choose the exact variation, sign in, enter a supported delivery address and request a shipping quote before Stripe checkout. Physical products do not include a tier roll, digital download or numbered-edition transfer. Current availability is checked through the quote; the initial integration supports US delivery in USD.

Can I request any studio artwork on canvas or apparel?

Use the contact form to send the artwork title or LWV serial and the product you want. LWVader will check print suitability, available Gelato product variations and pricing. A request does not guarantee that a product or destination can be supported.

Are these editions NFTs?

No. Numbered editions and their internal marketplace use the studio’s own ledger, not a blockchain. The NFT genre page remains available for separate release updates. Join studio updates to request NFT release notifications; do not treat a digital edition purchase as an NFT transfer.

Does verification stop copying?

No. Verification checks the source and integrity of the issued file and signed record. It does not stop screenshots, copying, editing or redistribution, and it does not erase files previously downloaded by another holder.

Are digital purchases refundable?

Because downloaded digital files cannot be returned, completed digital purchases are generally final except where required by law or when the delivered file is defective or materially different from its listing. Contact the studio about a delivery problem. See the Terms and the individual listing.

Does Pixel Shroom Studio claim copyright over purchased artwork?

Pixel Shroom Studio does not assert copyright restrictions over its purchased AI-generated artwork. Copyright treatment of AI-generated material can vary by country and may depend on the amount of human contribution. Pixel Shroom Studio does not promise that a buyer can obtain exclusive copyright protection.
