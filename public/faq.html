Forge & Print help

Your next step, explained.

Rolling, collecting, upgrading and printing—with your purchases together in My Vault.

What am I buying?

You choose the artwork, then buy an available roll. Verified payment assigns one of its finished uploaded variants. A digital-only roll includes the digital edition and signed receipt. A canvas-inclusive roll also includes one prepaid US canvas claim.

Can I choose my tier?

A primary roll assigns a tier after payment. You can see the six protected previews, dimensions, listing rules and supply before buying. Forge combines eligible editions into the stated next tier; it does not roll again.

Which offers are open?

Artwork pages check live availability. Digital Rolls are $8 and Canvas + Digital Rolls are $69 plus $13.49 US shipping before applicable tax when enabled. Older listings retain their displayed prices. Bundles and promotional free prints are not enabled in this release.

How does the Forge work?

Combine distinct eligible digital-only editions of the same artwork and tier: 5 Standard for 1 Silver; 3 Silver for 1 Gold; 3 Gold for 1 Emerald; 2 Emerald for 1 Sapphire; 2 Sapphire for 1 Prismatic. Confirm the exact inputs before permanent consumption. Authorized output stock and caps apply.

Which editions cannot be Forged?

Consumed, held, listed, printed or canvas-inclusive editions cannot be Forge inputs. An included canvas claim cannot be sacrificed in a digital-only upgrade. Your Vault checks eligibility; the server checks again before committing.

What happens after payment?

The server verifies payment and reserves one copy. A concealed card starts its continuous opening, then slows into the reveal. Your ready edition saves automatically in My Vault. The animation presents the server result and does not choose it.

What if payment, preparation or my connection is interrupted?

A paid allocation keeps the same copy, tier and edition number. Return to My Vault or retain your confirmation link. A Forge retry with the same operation returns its original output. Contact the studio if preparation needs attention; do not pay again to recover an existing edition.

What files will I receive?

One copy of the assigned uploaded image in its listed format and dimensions, plus a studio-signed JSON receipt. The private master is not delivered. Protected public previews are separate from delivered edition files. Download and back up both files from My Vault.

When is my canvas printed?

Gelato fulfills approved canvases. Printing is optional for digital-only editions. For a canvas-inclusive roll, the included product and US shipping are prepaid but production waits until you redeem the claim and confirm an address. Track the resulting order in My Vault. Delivery estimates depend on the approved product and destination.

Does forging include a free canvas?

No. A digital-only Forge upgrades the digital edition. Its canvas is purchased separately at the displayed product price, shipping and applicable tax. This release does not enable promotional free Prismatic claims.

What canvas does each tier offer?

Standard and Silver: 16 × 16 slim unframed. Gold: 16 × 16 thick unframed. Emerald: 16 × 20 portrait Black frame. Sapphire: 16 × 20 portrait Dark wood frame. Prismatic: 16 × 20 portrait Natural wood frame. US delivery only.

Are the odds fixed or on-chain?

Published percentages are base roll rates. Listing-specific caps and remaining inventory can change effective odds. Assignments use secure server-side randomness and the studio ledger, not a blockchain. Secure randomness alone is not publicly verifiable proof of fairness.

Do I need an account or a crypto wallet?

A studio account is required for numbered editions, Forge eligibility and private downloads. No crypto wallet is required. Use the password-reset email option on My Vault if you need account recovery.

What does verification establish?

An unchanged image and its signed receipt can be checked locally using SHA-256 against the studio public key. Verification establishes file identity and source integrity, not exclusivity, resale value or copy protection. Earlier direct-file purchases retain their original verification method.

Can I use or edit the artwork commercially?

Published personal and commercial permissions remain unchanged. Read Usage Rights for editing, reproduction, redistribution and resale. Preserve the issued image and receipt unchanged and edit a separate copy. Forging changes platform records; it cannot erase previously downloaded files.

How do I get help with downloads or a physical order?

Contact the studio with your order reference or artwork serial. Completed digital purchases follow the published Terms. Report damaged, incorrect or delayed physical products promptly; production and shipment may already be underway. Your applicable rights remain unchanged.
