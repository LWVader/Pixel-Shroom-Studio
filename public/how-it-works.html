The Forge & Print loop

Choose. Reveal.
Upgrade. Print.

Choose the artwork you love. Reveal a digital variant, build your collection and turn your chosen edition into canvas when you are ready.

  1. 1. Choose & pay

    Choose one artwork and an available digital or canvas-inclusive roll. Review its six variants, file sizes, price, supply and tier rules before secure payment.

  2. 2. Reveal & collect

    Verified payment triggers one server allocation. A continuous concealed opening reveals the result. Your ready edition saves automatically in My Vault. Retries do not reroll.

  3. 3. Forge or print

    Keep your edition, combine eligible digital-only editions of the same artwork and tier, or print your selected edition. A canvas-inclusive roll holds one prepaid print claim until you redeem it.

Two ways to start.

Digital Roll — $8

One assigned digital variant and signed receipt. Forge eligibility depends on ownership, edition history and output inventory. Canvas printing is a separate purchase.

Canvas + Digital — $69

One digital variant plus one included tier-mapped canvas claim. $13.49 prepaid US shipping: $82.49 before applicable tax. Redeem the canvas when ready. These editions cannot be Forge inputs.

Offers open only when the collection is ready and server checkout is enabled. Older purchases retain their original terms. Bundles and promotional free prints are not enabled in this release.

Combine editions into the next tier.

5 Standard → 1 Silver · 3 Silver → 1 Gold · 3 Gold → 1 Emerald · 2 Emerald → 1 Sapphire · 2 Sapphire → 1 Prismatic.

Inputs must be distinct eligible digital-only editions of the same artwork and tier. You review and confirm their permanent consumption. The output uses an authorized, prehashed copy of the uploaded tier image. Stock and global caps still apply; an unavailable output is never substituted.

Print the edition you choose.

Standard and Silver map to 16 × 16 slim unframed canvas; Gold to 16 × 16 thick unframed canvas. Emerald, Sapphire and Prismatic map to 16 × 20 portrait framed canvases in Black, Dark wood and Natural wood.

From My Vault, select an edition and review its dedicated print composition and delivery address. Digital-only prints use the displayed product price plus $13.49 US shipping and applicable tax. Included canvas claims have no second product or shipping charge. Production starts only after your print confirmation and valid payment or claim redemption.

Know the possibilities.

Six-tier base odds: Standard 60%, Silver 20%, Gold 10%, Emerald 6%, Sapphire 3%, Prismatic 1%. Listing rules and remaining caps apply. Exhausted tiers can change effective odds. A higher digital tier does not always change the physical format: Standard and Silver share slim canvas.

Identity you can check.

Each issued copy has a number, file fingerprint and signed receipt. Verification checks source and file integrity; purchases are nonexclusive unless stated otherwise. These editions use the studio ledger, not a blockchain.
