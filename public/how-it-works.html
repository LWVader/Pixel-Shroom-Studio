    Pick your art.

    Browse the nine collections. Every preview keeps its protection; listing details show source identity and recorded file dimensions.

    Pay, then reveal.

    For current numbered editions, verified payment triggers the server allocation and one continuous concealed reveal. The animation presents the result; it never rolls the tier.

Current physical products.

Mapped canvas and apparel products use their listed product variations and a delivery quote. Gelato receives a private print-ready copy after verified studio payment. It does not send the customer a digital original as part of that physical purchase.

Physical orders have their own account status page. A digital reveal does not imply that a Gelato shipment exists.

Identity with clear permissions.

Verification confirms the delivered file’s source and integrity. A serial does not establish exclusive ownership. Personal/commercial use, editing, redistribution and resale remain subject to the published usage rights.
