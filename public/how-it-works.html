From protected preview to your collection

Choose. Pay. Reveal. Collect.

One numbered edition, one server-assigned tier, one continuous reveal after verified payment.

01

Choose your artwork

Browse the newest 14 artworks on the homepage, the mixed-genre All artwork page, or any of the nine genre pages. For a numbered edition, review its price, tier images, image dimensions, initial odds, supply and caps. The protected preview is not the downloadable file.

02

Sign in and pay

Use your studio account and the listing’s Stripe checkout. Choose the artwork; a primary purchase does not let you pick its tier. The server verifies payment before allocating a prepared copy with a secure rarity roll.

03

Reveal your edition

The concealed card starts spinning after payment is confirmed. It keeps moving during preparation and image loading, then slows smoothly to reveal your tier. The animation displays the server result and never rerolls it. Reduced-motion settings are respected.

04

Save your files and collection

Select Add to my collection after reveal. The edition is saved to your account and the site requests both the exact image download and the signed JSON receipt. Allow multiple downloads if your browser asks, and keep secure backups.

05

Verify or use your artwork

Check the unchanged image and signed receipt on Verify an edition. Personal and commercial use, editing, redistribution and resale follow the existing Usage Rights. Keep the issued file unchanged and work from separate copies.

06

Trade an existing edition

When selling is available, eligible current holders can onboard with Stripe and list an edition from My collection. Marketplace buyers choose the already-revealed tier; resale transfers its recorded holder without a new roll.

Six finished images. One assigned edition.

LWVader uploads Standard, Silver, Gold, Emerald, Sapphire and Prismatic artwork before a new six-tier collection opens. Each authorized copy has its own prepared identity metadata and SHA-256 fingerprint. The released file preserves the finished image’s visible pixels; checkout does not generate frames or overlays. Follow the specific listing for older collections and its applicable odds.

The private master is retained by the studio. Limited numbered supply does not make the artwork exclusive or prevent copying.

Other ways to buy

Direct-file listings retain their stated private-download process and original-file verification. Canvas prints, apparel and gifts use a separate physical-product checkout: choose a mapped variation, request delivery pricing and pay through Stripe. Gelato receives your shipping details and a dedicated print-ready file for fulfillment. No digital download or rarity roll is included. Numbered digital editions are not NFTs; NFT release updates remain separate.
