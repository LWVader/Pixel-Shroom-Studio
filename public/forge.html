What consuming an image means.

The Forge requires distinct, currently owned images of the same artwork and tier. Items in a print claim, sale or another Forge operation cannot be used twice. You must review the inputs, output and any lost benefits before confirming an irreversible trade-up.

Consumed platform records remain in provenance history. Burning cannot erase files you already downloaded or revoke usage permissions already promised. A digital-only upgrade does not carry a $69 prepaid canvas entitlement.

Starting only with Standards takes 180 Standards to reach one Prismatic. Higher-tier pulls shorten the route. Output stock and authorized supply still apply.

This release accepts digital-only editions. Editions attached to canvas claims, print orders, active sales or payment reviews cannot be consumed.
