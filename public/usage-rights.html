Numbered editions and existing permissions

Your personal and commercial use, editing, redistribution and resale permissions below remain unchanged. A numbered edition is a copy of the assigned uploaded tier image, not delivery of the private master. Its limited supply concerns studio-issued numbered copies; it does not create exclusive rights to the design. A marketplace transfer changes the studio-recorded holder and future site download access, not the permissions already granted or files already downloaded.

Use the issued image and signed receipt together for edition verification. Editing or changing metadata changes the fingerprint. Direct-file purchases and any future NFT listing retain their own stated delivery and verification details.

Edition and purchase FAQ

Buyer information

Art you can use.

Read the studio’s usage terms before purchasing.

What can I do with my purchased artwork or NFT?

You may use your purchase for personal or commercial projects. You may display, print, publish, modify, resize, reproduce, incorporate, redistribute, or resell the artwork as you see fit. Pixel Shroom Studio does not require a separate usage license after purchase. Keep the studio-issued file unchanged with its receipt or verification record, and make separate copies for editing, printing, sharing, or AI enhancement.

Can I use the artwork commercially?

Yes. Purchased artwork and NFTs may be used in commercial projects, advertising, websites, publications, products, presentations, prints, and other work. Pixel Shroom Studio does not charge an additional commercial-use fee.

Can I modify, resize, or enhance the artwork?

Yes. You may crop, resize, recolor, convert, enhance, combine, or otherwise modify your purchase. Any changed version becomes a derivative file with a different digital fingerprint and will not verify as the unchanged issued file.

Can AI reproduce the artwork at a different size?

Yes. AI tools or conventional editing software may create another size or variation. Preserve the downloaded issued file separately because the new derivative will not retain the issued file’s valid SHA-256 fingerprint.

Can I redistribute or resell the artwork?

Yes. After purchase, you may redistribute or resell the artwork. Be aware that sharing the exact signed file also shares the same embedded authenticity information. The signature verifies the issued file; it does not identify its current holder.

Does purchasing an artwork give me exclusive rights?

No, unless the individual listing explicitly describes the sale as exclusive. Other customers may purchase or use copies of the same artwork. Cryptographic signing verifies source and integrity but does not create exclusivity.

Does Pixel Shroom Studio claim copyright over purchased artwork?

Pixel Shroom Studio does not assert copyright restrictions over its purchased AI-generated artwork. Copyright treatment of AI-generated material can vary by country and may depend on the amount of human contribution. Pixel Shroom Studio does not promise that a buyer can obtain exclusive copyright protection.

What makes my issued file verifiable?

A numbered edition has an edition number, prepared-copy SHA-256 fingerprint and studio-signed receipt. Earlier direct-file originals retain their LWV serial and verification record. Check the issued file using the verification method for its purchase type. These records confirm source and integrity, not exclusive rights.

What does the issued file provide me?

The issued file and its signed receipt or verification record let you check its studio source and file integrity. For a numbered edition, the receipt identifies its tier and edition number. Account collection records determine the current studio-recorded holder; a copied file or receipt alone does not transfer that record.

Should I preserve my issued file and receipt?

Yes. Keep the issued file unchanged and store at least one secure backup. Use separate copies for editing, printing, resizing, sharing, resale, or AI enhancement so the issued file remains verifiable.

Does the cryptographic signature prevent copying?

No. Cryptographic signing does not prevent copying, screenshots, redistribution, or modification. It provides a way to verify the source and integrity of an unchanged file.

Will my purchased image contain watermarks?

The storefront’s protective preview watermark is not added to the delivered purchase. A tier image retains any lettering and design elements present in the finished uploaded artwork. Check the listing for its intended appearance.
