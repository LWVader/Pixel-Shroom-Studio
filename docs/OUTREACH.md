# Finding the first Attention Pack customers

## Product Hunt, Indie Hackers and X

Look for independent launches where one striking visual could make the product easier to remember. Start with indie games, food and drink, electronic music, streetwear and creators. Match one campaign from the portfolio to the launch. Open with a detail that shows you looked at their project.

> Hey [first name], I saw the [specific thing] in your [project] launch. I’m Loren from Pixel Shroom Studio. I make five coordinated, surreal campaign images for indie launches for $199. I thought you might like [matched concept] because [one genuine connection]. Here’s the work if you’re planning launch visuals [portfolio link].

## LinkedIn

Post one fictional concept for a narrow niche. Lead with the image, explain the product goal, then explain how the central visual makes the product recognizable. Label the work as a fictional campaign.

> Fictional campaign for an independent coffee roaster. Orbit Roast needed a launch image for a midnight blend. I turned one coffee bean into an asteroid hovering over a violet city. You can still tell what’s being sold in a thumbnail. If you’re launching a drink and want the whole campaign to feel like one world, my Attention Pack includes five coordinated visuals for $199. [Portfolio link]

## After the first five to ten customers

Ask which image they actually used and where it ran. Use those answers to decide whether to enable the $199 landing page add-on or the $49 monthly backgrounds. Ask before sharing customer work as a case study.
